A few little details.

Can I place a real order here?

This is a cake-shop concept. You can build a basket and preview an order, but payments, pickup scheduling, and actual orders aren’t connected yet. No money is collected.

What about allergies and dietary needs?

The example menu includes wheat, eggs, and dairy. Ingredients and cross-contact information must be confirmed with the bakery before placing a real order. The custom cake form includes space for dietary requirements.

Can I add a personal message?

Use “Dream up a cake” to describe the message, colours, flavours, and occasion you have in mind. You can preview the request here without sending it.
